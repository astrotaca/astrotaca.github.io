Before you order

Which size do I need?

Measure across the outside of your dew shield where the panel will sit, not the clear opening. Each panel lists the range it fits, and its page has a size check. Still unsure? Send me your telescope model and I'll tell you which one.

Shipping and taxes

Orders ship from Norway. Shipping options and any taxes for your address are shown at checkout, before you pay. Outside Norway, your country may charge import VAT or customs fees on delivery.

Returns

You can cancel within 14 days of receiving your order, without giving a reason. Let me know through the contact form, then send the panel back within 14 days. I refund the price, including the original shipping, within 14 days. Return shipping is paid by you. The terms of sale have the details and a cancellation form.

Warranty

If your panel develops a fault, contact me and I'll repair or replace it. Norwegian consumer law gives you at least 2 years to report faults, and up to 5 years for products made to last much longer. See the terms of sale for what you can ask for.

Software

The panel connects over USB-C and works with NINA through ASCOM, or with the AstroTaca Windows app and the button on the panel. See how it works.
